# HELION art provenance

Art direction: Phase (GPT-6 Astra), September 2026. `solar_sky.png` was generated using the built-in **imagegen** tool, text-to-image mode, without reference images. The tool did not report a specific model version. The original 1536×1024 PNG is retained here; `helion/tools/pack_assets.py` compiles it to a 320×240 DAC-format environment plate. The title is locally typeset from Georgia; no font file is distributed. Effect textures and all animation are generated by C code.

## Exact sky prompt

Use case stylized-concept. A single finished panoramic cosmic environmental painting for HELION, a demoscene film on a microcontroller. Landscape 1536x1024. Immense solar nebula in deep black-violet space. Elegant sweeping curtains of copper, burnt orange, dark rose and luminous pale gold plasma frame a mostly empty dark central opening. Brightest light on the far left and upper right edges; the central sixty percent must stay dark and uncluttered for a real-time animated star sculpture. Bottom quarter near black, sparse deep purple dust, no ground. Vast cinematic scale, painterly science-fiction matte painting with flowing delicate filaments and restrained starlight, luxurious dramatic chiaroscuro, strong large readable shapes for a 320x240 screen. Not a photo of a known astronomical object. No planets, circular suns, black-hole rings, mechanical structures, people, spaceships, typography, symbols, logos, border or watermark. Warm molten gold against amethyst shadows, beautiful and mysterious. Fill edge to edge.
